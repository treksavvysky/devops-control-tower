CLAUDE DESIGN PROJECT — DEVOPS CONTROL TOWER
=============================================

ENTRY 1: PROJECT BLURB
----------------------

DevOps Control Tower (DCT) is a command center for AI-assisted software
delivery. It turns fuzzy intent into audited, deterministic work: tasks are
submitted, validated against policy, executed by agents, proven with evidence,
and gated by human review before anything lands. The dashboard is the human
window into that pipeline — an operator console where you monitor active
agents, watch runs stream in, inspect evidence packs (checks passed, verdicts,
artifacts), and make the final approve/reject call on agent work.

The visual language matches the mission: a dark, glassmorphic "control room"
aesthetic — deep near-black backgrounds, translucent blurred panels, Inter
type, and four semantic accents that carry meaning everywhere (blue =
action/running, green = approved/passed, amber = pending/needs-changes, red =
rejected/failed). The UI should feel calm and authoritative under load: dense
with signal, never noisy, with status always legible at a glance — because
every screen ultimately answers one question: is this work safe to ship?


ENTRY 2: BRAND / STYLE NOTES
----------------------------

Brand image: Mission control, flight-deck, operator-grade. Calm authority.
Precision without coldness. Trustworthy automation with a human hand on the
final lever. High signal, zero noise.

Style & mood: Dark-mode only. Glassmorphism — frosted translucent panels
(backdrop-blur), soft 16px radii, deep layered shadows, subtle radial glows in
blue and green at screen edges. Quiet surfaces, luminous accents.
Gradient-filled action buttons with faint colored glow. Pill-shaped uppercase
status badges. Subtle motion: gentle fade-in on page load, restrained hover
lift only on interactive cards. Nothing bounces, nothing shouts.

Typography: Inter. Tight-tracked bold headlines with a white-to-gray gradient
fade; regular-weight body; small uppercase letter-spaced labels for statuses
and metadata. Big confident stat numbers (2.5rem bold) in accent colors.

Color palette:
- Background: near-black blue-gray #0f1115
- Surfaces: translucent charcoal rgba(25,28,36,0.65) over blur; hairline
  borders rgba(255,255,255,0.08)
- Text: soft white #f0f2f5, secondary gray #9da3ad, muted #6b7280
- Accents (strictly semantic): electric blue #3b82f6 = primary/action/running;
  emerald #10b981 = success/approved; amber #f59e0b = warning/pending review;
  red #ef4444 = danger/rejected
- Accents appear as solid gradients on buttons, 15%-opacity tints with
  matching borders on badges and callouts

Feel to avoid: playful, pastel, skeuomorphic, corporate-beige, cluttered
dashboards, neon cyberpunk excess.
